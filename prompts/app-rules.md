Drawing apps (无边记/Freeform, 备忘录/Notes, Markup): before drawing, tap the pen or pencil tool in the toolbar so it is selected, and check it is highlighted; without a drawing tool a drag only scrolls or moves the canvas. Then draw with path or circle. A button that did nothing after two taps is not the one you want: look at the other controls and try another. A system alert asking for permission during a task (allow paste, photos, microphone, notifications, location): tap its allow button (允许 / 允许粘贴 / Allow) once and go on; never switch between its buttons.
Drawing a face or any shape made of parts: first choose the center (cx, cy) and radius r of the outline, then place every part relative to it and inside it. x and y units differ (both 0-1000 of the screen's width and height), so r in y units is r * width / height (about r * 0.46 on a portrait iPhone). A smiley: circle cx cy r; eyes at (cx - 0.35r, cy - 0.3r_y) and (cx + 0.35r, cy - 0.3r_y), each a circle of radius about 0.12r and never under 25 (smaller ones come out as a faint dot); the mouth a path from (cx - 0.45r, cy + 0.2r_y) through (cx, cy + 0.5r_y) to (cx + 0.45r, cy + 0.2r_y). Check the result: every part must be inside the outline. Draw all the parts in one reply (several DO lines), then look once. A part you can see counts as drawn, even if it is small or thin: do not draw it again, and never redraw the outline. If one part is really missing, draw only that part once more, a little larger.
Drawing tools: tap the pen or pencil once; it is selected when it stands raised or highlighted. Tapping the selected tool again only opens its thickness and colour panel: if that panel is showing, tap an empty spot of the canvas to close it, and do not keep tapping the tool.
A drawing app often reopens the last board or note, with earlier drawings on it. When the goal is to draw something new, that old content is not the result: go back to the list (the back arrow at the top left) and create a new board or note first.
